About Our NGO

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization established in 2020. The organization works towards education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability, community support, and skill development. Through dedicated volunteers and social initiatives, the foundation creates positive impact across India.

Our Ongoing Projects

```

Project Bachpanshala

Providing education support, digital literacy, and life skills training for underprivileged children.

Project Vikas

Skill development and internship opportunities to improve employability and career growth.

Project Udaan

Empowering women through entrepreneurship, financial literacy, and skill development programs.

Project Jeev

Supporting animal welfare through rescue, feeding, protection, and awareness campaigns.

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental conservation through tree plantation and sustainability initiatives.

Project Seva

Food distribution, clothing support, blood donation drives, and community welfare activities.
```
